Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Super Spy Guard helps users check rooms and nearby devices for safety. The app does not require an account.

Data We Use

The app may use the camera, microphone, Bluetooth, local network, location, and motion-related sensors when the user starts a scan or opens a related tool.

Scan results, checklist entries, and history are saved on the user's device. The app does not send this data to our server.

Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is used only for checks started by the user, such as light reflection checks and sound checks. The app does not upload photos, video, or audio.

Bluetooth, Local Network, and Location

Bluetooth and local network access are used to show nearby devices and network information. Location may be used to attach a place to scan history. This data stays on the device.

Sharing

The app does not sell personal data. The app does not share personal data with advertisers.
